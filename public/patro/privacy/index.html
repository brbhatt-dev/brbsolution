Privacy Policy — BR Patro (बिआर पात्रो : नेपाली पात्रो र पञ्चाङ्ग)

Package Name: com.br.patro  |  Developer: Bhakta Raj Bhatt (BRB)  |  Effective Date: October 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Core Privacy Summary (Zero Personal Data Selling & Zero Ads): BR Patro (com.br.patro) is an offline-first Nepali Bikram Sambat Calendar, Vedic Panchang, Kundali, Sankalpa, and Karmakanda application. We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal user data, we do not display third-party advertisements (Zero Ads), and all personal notes, reminders, and Kundali profiles are stored strictly locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Processed

BR Patro is engineered to function primarily offline on your Android device. Below is a complete breakdown of data categories handled by the application:

  • No Account or Personal Identity Required: You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, phone number, email address, or contact list to use BR Patro.
  • User-Created Notes, Kundali & Sankalpa Profiles (100% On-Device): Any daily calendar notes, reminders, saved birth charts (Janma Kundali), or Sankalpa preferences (such as Gotra or family names) entered by the user are stored exclusively in the device's local application storage (localStorage / SharedPreferences) and are never uploaded to any external server.
  • Optional Location Data (Ephemeral, On-Device Calculation Only): If you explicitly grant location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) when using the local Sunrise/Sunset, Kundali birthplace, or Sankalpa location feature, your GPS coordinates are processed ephemerally inside the device to calculate astronomical solar/lunar timings and local city coordinates. Location coordinates are never stored on our servers or shared with third parties.
  • Push Notifications & Anonymous Version Diagnostics: To deliver festival alerts and community broadcast announcements via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and to verify app version stability, the app uses a standard FCM installation token and an anonymous random installation identifier (UUID) with the app version number. This technical telemetry contains zero personally identifiable information (Zero PII).

3. Data Retention and User Data Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play's Data Safety and Data Deletion requirements:

  • Instant On-Device Deletion: Because all personal notes, saved Kundalis, and preferences are stored locally on your device, you can delete individual notes or saved Kundali charts directly inside the app at any time using the delete buttons, clear application storage via Android Settings → Apps → BR Patro → Storage → Clear Storage, or uninstall the app to permanently erase all local data.
  • Zero Server-Side Personal Accounts: BR Patro does not maintain user accounts or store personal profiles on external servers. If you have any questions or deletion inquiries, you may email info@brbhatta.com.

4. Third-Party Services & Security

  • Zero Advertising SDKs: BR Patro does not display advertisements and does not collect or share the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) for marketing or tracking.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): Used solely to deliver optional push notifications for Nepali festivals and important calendar updates over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Encryption in Transit: All optional network requests (broadcast JSON, radio streams, FCM) are transmitted securely over HTTPS (TLS).

5. Children's Privacy & Family Safety

BR Patro provides cultural, educational, and Vedic calendar reference information suitable for general audiences and families. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).
