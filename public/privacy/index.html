1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Land Solution ("the Application", package name: com.brbhatta.landsolution), developed by Bhakta Raj Bhatt, handles your information when you use the Android mobile application, Desktop application, or web tools accessible via https://brbhatta.com.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

The Application requests only the minimal runtime permissions required to perform land survey and engineering operations:

  • Precise & Coarse Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION):
    Required exclusively during active field survey sessions (such as "Walk & Measure", "Stake Out", and "My Land GPS") to compute perimeter lengths, plot boundaries, and geodetic coordinates (Everest 1830 MUTM & WGS84). Location access occurs strictly in the foreground while the feature is open. The app does NOT request or use background location tracking (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION).
  • Camera (CAMERA):
    Required optionally when you choose to photograph a boundary marker, landmark waypoint, or land ownership deed (Lalpurja) for local record archiving and OCR. Camera access is strictly user-initiated, and photos are saved only to your device's local sandbox storage.
  • Storage & File Access:
    The app uses the standard Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) and file picker to let you import and export ESRI Shapefiles (.shp, .dbf), AutoCAD (.dxf), GeoTIFFs, and PDF survey reports. The app does not request broad MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions.
  • Internet (INTERNET):
    Used to download map tiles (OpenStreetMap / Esri Satellite) when viewing the map, and to open official governmental portals (such as the Nepal Department of Survey at dos.gov.np) via your external browser.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE):
    Provides haptic confirmation when a GPS coordinate fix or stakeout target tolerance is reached.

3. Data Collection, Sharing & Third Parties

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, register, or provide personal credentials to use Land Solution.
  • No Advertising SDKs: The application contains zero advertising frameworks, tracking SDKs, or analytics trackers.
  • No Third-Party Sale: No personal or survey data is shared, sold, or distributed to any commercial entity.
  • Online Maps: When viewing satellite or street maps, standard tile requests are sent to public map tile providers (e.g., OpenStreetMap, CARTO, Esri) using standard HTTPS requests, which process network traffic under their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

All survey data, saved plots, waypoint histories, and cadastral calculations are stored locally in the application's private sandbox storage on your device. You have full control over your data:

  • You can delete any saved plot, survey record, or waypoint directly within the app's respective management screen.
  • Uninstalling the application or selecting "Clear Storage" in your Android system settings permanently erases all locally saved records.

5. Children's Privacy

Land Solution is a professional geomatics, civil engineering, and land administration tool designed for surveyors, engineers, and adults. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
