Privacy Policy — Land Solution
Updated: September 30, 2026
Developer: BRB / Bhakta Raj Bhatt

Local records
Survey projects, Lalpurja records and settings are stored on your device. The app does not add encryption to these records. Device security and operating-system backup settings apply. Delete records using available app controls or clear app data in Android settings. Exported/shared copies and operating-system backups must be managed separately.

Location and online maps
Location permission supports GPS surveying, maps and navigation. Online map providers (including OpenStreetMap, Google, Esri and CARTO) receive your IP address and requested map tile locations. Route requests send start and destination coordinates to OSRM. Address searches send entered search text to Nominatim. Approximate location lookup uses ipwho.is and exposes your IP address. These services process requests under their own policies. GPS access is intended for foreground surveying; no background-location permission is requested.

Photos and documents
Camera and file selection are used when you choose photos or import survey documents. Cloud AI scanning is disabled in production builds. Manual entry and pasted text remain available. In developer/debug builds configured for AI scanning, a separate confirmation is required before sending the full selected photo, potentially including names and land ownership details, to Google Gemini. Google processes uploaded data under its applicable service terms. Only upload documents you are authorized to share.

Files and sharing
Reports, backups and exported drawings are created on your device. When you share them, the selected destination receives those files. You control sharing and deletion of exported copies.

Other network requests
Online features require internet access. Font downloads used by reports or the interface may contact Google font services. Third-party servers can receive connection metadata such as IP address. The app has no advertising SDK declared in its dependencies.

Children and changes
This app is intended for surveying and engineering users, not children under 13. Policy changes will be included in app updates and the published policy page. Contact the developer through the website above for privacy questions.
