HOW TO PREVIEW LOCALLY RIGHT NOW:
---------------------------------
Double-click "start_website.bat" or the desktop shortcut "Launch Land Solution Web".
It will automatically open your browser at:
  http://localhost:8080

You can also open it on your mobile phone connected to the same Wi-Fi using the IP shown in the console!


HOW TO DEPLOY WHEN YOU PURCHASE YOUR NEW WEBSITE / DOMAIN:
---------------------------------------------------------
When you buy your domain (e.g. www.yourlandnepal.com) and web hosting,
all you have to do is upload these files:

METHOD 1: cPanel / DirectAdmin / Shared Hosting (Hostinger, Namecheap, Bluehost, etc.)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Log into your hosting cPanel or control panel.
2. Open "File Manager" and go into the "public_html" folder.
3. Click "Upload" and upload the zip file:
   "Land_Solution_Web_Package.zip" (saved on your Desktop).
4. Right-click the uploaded zip file and select "Extract".
5. Make sure the files (index.html, main.dart.js, assets, canvaskit, .htaccess) are directly inside "public_html".
6. Open your domain (e.g. https://yourlandnepal.com) in your browser.
   Your Land Solution app is LIVE worldwide!

METHOD 2: Free Cloud Hosting (Netlify / Vercel / Firebase Hosting)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Netlify: Go to app.netlify.com, drag and drop this entire "Land_Solution_Web_Site" folder onto the page. Your site is live in 10 seconds!
- Firebase: Run 'firebase deploy --only hosting'.
